Privacy Policy for LayerCraft

Effective Date: November 8, 2026

At LayerCraft, accessible from the LayerCraft mobile application, one of our main priorities is the privacy of our users. This Privacy Policy document outlines the types of information that is not collected, used, or stored by LayerCraft.

1. Data Collection and Storage

LayerCraft is built with an offline-first architecture.

Local Storage Only: All user data, including custom filament spools, calculation history, and app preferences, are stored strictly locally on your device using secure local key-value storage (Hive).

No Cloud Transmission: We do not collect, transmit, sync, or back up any of your personal data or print metrics to external servers or cloud databases.

2. Analytics and Tracking

LayerCraft does not use any third-party analytics SDKs, advertising networks, or user tracking tools.

We do not track your usage behavior, location, or device identifiers.

3. Permissions

Storage / File Access: The app requests storage or sharing permissions solely for the purpose of generating and exporting local PDF client quotes to your device.

4. Children’s Privacy

LayerCraft does not knowingly collect any personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13.

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Thus, you are advised to review this page periodically for any changes.
